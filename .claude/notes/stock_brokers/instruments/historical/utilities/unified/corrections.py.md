# corrections.py

## Why one series stepping alone decides a pair step

Until 2026-10-29 a pair step with two plausible readings and no Yahoo event was always given to NSE and left provisional, since flattrade's NSE series are the ones known to be served pre-adjusted. A provisional row is never applied, so JBCHEPHARM's NSE series stayed at half its price before its 2023-09-18 split, and V12 failed on it every night: Flattrade's BSE closes fell from 2,955.80 to 1,483.05 that day while its NSE closes went from 1,478.47 to 1,484.70. Yahoo lists no split for JBCHEPHARM, and Flattrade's intraday bars start in September 2025.

The prices alone do decide it. A broker adjusting for a split or bonus divides the older prices, so the series it served adjusted shows no move on the ex-date, the raw series moves by the factor, and the correction multiplies by more than 1. `one_series_stepped` accepts a reading only when its multiplier is above 1, the series it corrects moved by less than `UNMOVED_TOLERANCE` (5% in log terms, an ordinary day) and the other moved by the step within `STEPPED_TOLERANCE` (10%). A multiplier below 1 means a consolidation, which still needs a Yahoo event. The row still has to pass the existing confirmation rules (a simple ratio within `SNAP_TOLERANCE`, `MINIMUM_NEWER_RUN` days on the newer side, and not dated inside a hole), so the rule only decides the side.

Two looser forms were tried against the 53 provisional cross-exchange rows on 2026-10-29 and rejected. Requiring only that the common move be near the factor, as the Yahoo branch measures it, decided 42 of them, including small fractions such as 9/7 in thinly traded stocks where both prices swung by 25 to 40% on the day, and TVVISION corrected three times in opposite directions. Requiring one series to be flat within 3% without the multiplier condition confirmed FCSSOFT, RADAAN and SABEVENTS at 5/6 and 6/7, which would mean consolidations no company made.

A read-only run of the builder over the 155 instruments V12 flagged, with `store` replaced so nothing was written, found the same 70 cross-exchange rows with both versions; 19 were confirmed before and 27 after. The 8 newly confirmed are ABSLBANETF, ABSLNN50ET, NIF100IETF (each 10), BSLGOLDETF (100), BSLSENETFG (81/8), MOLOWVOL and MOMOMENTUM (5) and JBCHEPHARM (2). BSLSENETFG's step of 10.072 snaps to 81/8 rather than 10, because `snap` takes a larger denominator when it fits clearly better; that leaves its older prices about 1.25% high instead of ten times low, and is left for a separate change to `snap`.
